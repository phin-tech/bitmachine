The first BitMachine: a classic skinnable player for Apple Music.

- **The Main Window**, drawn from a classic `.wsz` skin: transport, time, scrolling title, volume, balance and seek, with Double Size (⌃D) and Windowshade (⌃W). Load a skin with File ▸ Open Skin… (⇧⌘S); the built-in skin is a placeholder for now.
- **Apple Music**: search the catalog or your library in the Library window (⌘L, or the Eject button) and double-click to play. Tracks play one after another from the Playlist.
- **Volume, balance and a real spectrum analyzer for Apple Music.** BitMachine plays Apple Music's audio through its own engine. It asks for System Audio Recording to do it (System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Screen & System Audio Recording); without it, Apple Music still plays, but BitMachine's volume and visualizer can't reach it.
