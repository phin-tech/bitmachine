- **Navidrome and other Subsonic servers.** Sign in under Settings ▸ Servers. BitMachine signs in with a token rather than sending your password, which it keeps in your Keychain.
- In the Library (⌘L), the Servers tab browses a server by artist and album, and searches it when you press Return. Server songs play through the same equalizer, volume and visualizer as everything else, with pause and seeking.
- The next server song is fetched while the current one plays, and songs you've played are kept on disk for a while, so they start at once and play even when the server can't be reached. Formats macOS can't play, such as Ogg, come from the server as MP3.
- **Server Playlists.** Open one from the Library's Server Playlists menu; its songs take the Playlist's place. Rearrange them, then LIST ▸ Save to Server Playlist writes them back. Tracks from other sources stay in your Playlist but aren't saved to the server.
