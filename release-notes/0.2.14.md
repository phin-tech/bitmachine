- **Control another BitMachine.** Turn on Settings ▸ Remote Control ▸ "Allow other Macs to control this BitMachine" on one Mac, then choose File ▸ Control Another BitMachine… on another. The first time, the first Mac shows a four-digit code to type. While you're controlling, your windows show the other Mac's song, time, visualizer, volume, equalizer and Playlist, and every control works it: play, pause, skip, seek, volume, shuffle and repeat, the equalizer, and Playlist edits such as removing, reordering and sorting. Songs you add from the Library or the Radio go to its Playlist. Your own Playlist and settings come back when you stop.
- **Local Files stay put.** Your Mac's own audio files can't be sent to the other Mac, so they're left out, with a notice.
