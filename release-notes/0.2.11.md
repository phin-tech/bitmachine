- **Browse your Apple Music library.** In the Library (⌘L), My Library now has artist and album columns like My Files: pick an artist for all their songs, album by album, or an album for just its songs. Search your library by pressing Return.
- **Open Apple Music Playlists.** The Apple Music Playlists menu under My Library puts a playlist's songs in the Playlist, asking first if that would replace anything. Your changes stay in BitMachine; the Apple Music Playlist doesn't change.
