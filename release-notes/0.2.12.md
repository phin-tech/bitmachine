- **Save as Apple Music Playlist.** LIST ▸ Save as Apple Music Playlist… makes a new playlist in your Apple Music library from the Playlist's Apple Music songs, in order. Tracks from other sources stay in your Playlist and are left out, with a notice. A new playlist can take a minute to show up in Music.
- **Playlist Files.** LIST ▸ Save Playlist File… and Open Playlist File…, or drop an .m3u8 or .m3u on BitMachine. Files are extended M3U8: other players can read their local files and radio stations, and BitMachine brings back everything, Apple Music and server songs included.
- **Playlist Windowshade.** The Playlist's shade button, or a double-click on its title bar, rolls it up to a thin bar showing the current song and its time; drag the bar's grip to change its width.
