- **Your own music files.** Add Files… and Add Folder… in the Playlist's ADD menu, or drop files and folders on the Playlist (adds them) or the Main Window (adds them and plays). Opening files with BitMachine works too. MP3, AAC/M4A, ALAC, FLAC, WAV and AIFF; titles, artists and albums come from the files' tags.
- Local files go through the same equalizer, balance, volume and visualizer as everything else, with pause, seeking and their real bitrate and sample rate on the display.
- **Internet radio, early.** View ▸ Radio (⌥R) finds stations and keeps six on preset buttons; a station shows its frequency, signal and what's playing on the Main Window. It docks with the other windows.
