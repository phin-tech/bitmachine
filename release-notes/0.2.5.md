- **The Playlist Window**, drawn from your skin and docked under the Equalizer (or the Main Window). Open it with the Main Window's PL button or View ▸ Playlist (⌥E).
  - Double-click a song to play it; click, ⇧-click and ⌘-click to select; drag to reorder; Delete removes; Return plays; the arrow keys move.
  - ADD finds music, REM removes (selected, everything else, unavailable songs, or all), SEL selects, MISC sorts by title, artist or album, reverses or shuffles the order, and LIST starts a new playlist.
  - The mini transport and time are in the bottom-right corner, with the length of your selection and of the whole playlist.
  - Drag the bottom-right corner to make it bigger; it remembers its size.
- The Library window is now just for finding music.
