- **The Equalizer.** A ten-band equalizer with a preamp, drawn from your skin, docked under the Main Window: open it with the Main Window's EQ button or View ▸ Equalizer (⌥G). Drag a slider to boost or cut a band; ON turns it on, and PRESETS has a few starting points (Bass Boost, Treble Boost, Loudness, Vocal, Late Night). It works on Apple Music too, and BitMachine remembers your settings.
- Windowshade the Equalizer from its title bar (double-click, or its button).
